Discovery Page:

The **empty space is too passive** right now. For Quiet Luxury, I wouldn’t fill it with decoration—I’d use it to create a **larger editorial composition**.

### Better use of the space

* Make the left content column **~45–50% wider**.
* Move the whole composition slightly upward and let it occupy more horizontal space.
* Increase the question to around **40–48px** and give it a stronger editorial presence.
* Make the option cards **larger and more spacious**, perhaps 3 across with generous padding.
* Let the text input become wider and taller, almost like a **personal letter/note field**.
* Make the right dossier panel slightly wider and taller, with more breathing room between sections.
* Introduce a large, extremely subtle background phrase such as:
  **THE ART OF TRAVEL**

  at very low opacity—almost watermark-like.
* Or use a faint oversized **“02”** behind the composition. This fills space without making the page busy.
* Add a small vertical editorial line extending from the question down through the interaction.

```text

                                      ┌───────────────┐
                                      │ TRAVELER      │

                                      │               │
                                      │ Context       │
                                      │ Preferences   │

                                      │ Assumptions   │
                                      └───────────────┘
```

The important change is **scale**. Right now the interface occupies maybe 35% of the available visual field. I'd bring it closer to **55–60%**, while keeping the remaining whitespace around it.

That will preserve the Quiet Luxury feeling while making the page feel **intentional rather than unfinished**.
